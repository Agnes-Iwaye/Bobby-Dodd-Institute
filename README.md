# Bobby-Dodd-Institute - Stakeholder & Inventory Management 

Stakeholder Engagement and Government Relations Program

Industry: Nonprofit, Public Affairs, and Government Relations
Role: Program Manager / Advocacy and Stakeholder Engagement Lead

I developed a structured stakeholder-management program for Bobby Dodd Institute, including stakeholder identification, influence-interest mapping, communication planning, engagement tracking, legislative outreach, site visits, and follow-up. The initiative strengthened relationships with government officials and supported formal recognition of the organization through a Georgia House resolution.

Skills demonstrated: Stakeholder mapping, communication planning, RACI, advocacy, relationship management, executive briefing, CRM management, and program reporting.
